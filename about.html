About the Project & Team

This website was developed as an academic project for 1st Year B.Tech Engineering students to demonstrate mastery of standard, semantic HTML5 structure, form handling, tables, and responsive mobile viewport principles without relying on heavy frameworks or external scripts.

Summary of HTML Concepts Demonstrated

  • Semantic Layout Tags: <header>, <nav>, <main>, <section>, <article>, <footer>.
  • Form Controls: <fieldset>, <legend>, <input type="text">, <input type="email">, <input type="tel">, <input type="date">, <input type="radio">, <select>, <textarea>.
  • Tabular Data: <table>, <thead>, <tbody>, <tr>, <th>, <td> for specifications and schedules.
  • Interactive Elements without JS: <details> and <summary> for collapsible accordions.
  • Protocols & Links: Relative page linking, anchor targets, mailto: for email, and tel: for calling.
