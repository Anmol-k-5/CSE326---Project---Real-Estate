Frequently Asked Financial Questions

Common queries regarding home loans, tax incentives, and registration rules in India.

Under Section 80C, borrowers can claim deductions up to ₹1.5 Lakh per financial year on principal repayment. Under Section 24(b), deductions up to ₹2.0 Lakh can be claimed on interest paid for a self-occupied property. First-time buyers can also explore additional deductions under Section 80EEA subject to statutory limits.
Floating interest rates fluctuate according to RBI's Repo-Linked Lending Rate (RLLR) over time, meaning your EMI or tenure automatically adjusts when rates change. Fixed interest rates remain constant for an initial period (usually 2-5 years) providing predictable installments. Floating rate home loans have zero prepayment penalty under RBI mandates.
Standard requirements include PAN card, Aadhaar card, past 6 months bank account statements, salary slips for the last 3 months (or 3 years ITR & audited balance sheets for self-employed individuals), Form 16, and the builder's RERA registration certificate.
Yes, stamp duty and registration fees paid during the acquisition year can be claimed as a tax deduction under Section 80C within the overall annual ceiling of ₹1.5 Lakh.
